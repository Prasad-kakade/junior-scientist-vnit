MODEL-O-THON

Carnival of the creators: The Epic model making showdown

Junior Scientist presents Model-o-thon — a platform for young children to showcase their creative skills and knowledge through a single project — a model! Transform your imagination into reality, fostering practical concepts, creativity, and design skills while rediscovering the joy of learning through hands-on experience in model making — a fundamental step in scientific exploration !!

Event Structure

Junior scientist provides a platform for young talents to rediscover creativity with the most fundamental and efficient starting step-model making.

  • Team Limit: The participating team should consist of NOT more than 3 members.
  • Format: All types of models (working and non-working) are acceptable and will be evaluated on the same grounds.
  • Presentation: Use of charts and paperwork for presentation is advisable.
  • Rounds: The event will consist of one round.

Competition Flow

  • 01Teams will be required to conceptualize their models in detail and deliver a presentation to the panel of judges. Judging time: 3 minutes per team.
  • 02The judges will ask questions regarding the working and applications of the models to test the conceptual understanding of the participants.
  • 03The team that stands out will win the competition!

Event Themes

5-8

Trash to Treasure

Trash is just a treasure waiting to be built !!

Examples: Solar oven from a pizza box, Plastic spoon water wheel, Self watering plastic bottle planter, Rubber band powered rover, etc

9-12

Tech for the planet

Design.Build.Green

Examples: gravity based water harvesting systems, waste segregation, renewable energy, natural cooling systems, etc

Note: The event will consist of one round.

Judging Criteria

1. Originality
2. Creativity
3. Conceptual knowledge
4. Impact on the environment and society
5. Presentation skills
