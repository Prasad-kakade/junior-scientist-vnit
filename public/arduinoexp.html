EVENT DESCRIPTION

ARDUINO Expo 2026 is a premier school-level technology exhibition dedicated to nurturing innovation, creativity, and technical excellence among young minds. Brings ideas to life by building the future with Electronics, Embedded Systems, Robotics, Internet of Things (IoT), Artificial Intelligence (AI), and Automation. It encourages students to think creatively, solve real world problems and showcase their technical skills.

COMPETITION CATEGORIES

EXPLORER

CLASSES 5th - 8th

The Explorer category introduces students to the fundamentals of electronics, embedded systems, programming, sensors, and automation.

INNOVATOR

CLASSES 9th - 12th

Participants are encouraged to explore & integrate Multiple Electronic Components, AI, IoT, Robotics, Automation, Mobile & Web Apps, Cloud Computing, Custom PCBs, and CAD tools.

COMPETITION THEMES

EXPLORER THEMES

  • Smart Home & Daily Life
  • Environment & Safety
  • Robotics & Automation

Examples: Automatic Street Light, Smart Dustbin, Smart doorbell, Rain alarm, Automatic plant watering system, Obstacle Avoiding System.

INNOVATOR THEMES

  • Sustainable & Smart Technologies
  • Advanced Embedded Systems
  • Intelligent Systems & Advanced Robotics

Examples: Gesture Controlled Robot, Robotic Arm, Automatic Sorting Machine, Autonomous Robot, Smart Waste Management, Smart Irrigation.
