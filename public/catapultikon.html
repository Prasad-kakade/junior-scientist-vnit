The Enchanted Scroll

For centuries, the world of magic has been filled with powerful spells, legendary wizards, and extraordinary feats. But today, a new challenge awaits.

Hidden within the walls of the wizarding world lies an ancient magical artefact — the Enchanted Catapult. Only those who can master its power, speed, and precision can prove themselves worthy of the title of Master Wizard.

Today, you are no longer ordinary competitors. You are wizards entering the ultimate magical trial.

✦ ✧ THEME: HARRY PORTER✧ ✦

The Three Trials

Round 1 — The Wizard's Trial

Aim: Range

Every wizard must first prove the power and reach of their magical catapult. Each team will receive 3 attempts, and the best attempt will be considered. The maximum range achieved will determine the ranking, and the top 50 percent teams will advance to the next magical trial.

"Every great wizard begins with a first spell. Every great champion begins with a first launch."

Round 2 — The Spellcasting Trial

Aim: Frequency

The surviving wizards now face the ultimate test of speed and consistency. Each wizard will be given a certain amount of time to launch as many projectiles as possible. The number of successful launches within the given time will be recorded. Wizards who demonstrate the highest frequency of successful shots will qualify for the final round.

"The clock is ticking, the spells are flying — only the fastest wizards will survive."

Round 3 — Grand Finale

Aim: Range + Accuracy

The final wizards enter the ultimate magical showdown. The target will be placed at the maximum successful range achieved in the previous rounds. Each finalist will have 3 chances to hit the Target. The challenge is to combine Range (Reach the target) and Accuracy (Hit the target successfully).

"The final spell is cast. The target awaits. Only one wizard will claim the crown."
