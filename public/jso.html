Event Description

Junior Science Olympiad is a platform to challenge your scientific knowledge and analytical skills. Through two exciting rounds, participants compete, learn, and showcase their passion for science.

Event Structure

ELIMINATION ROUND

Pen and paper examination featuring application based questions (MCQ, Integer type, Match the columns, etc.)

FINAL ROUND

This will be a non disclosed round which will provide a surprise element.

Age Groups

MINDSPARK 5th, 6th, 7th Class
MASTERMIND 8th, 9th, 10th Class
TEAM SIZE INDIVIDUAL PARTICIPATION

Important Dates & Venue

ROUND 1 4th October Pen and Paper Test
ROUND 2 11th October Surprise Round
VENUE VNIT NAGPUR Main Campus
